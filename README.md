An entry for the make.fun bounty **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office
Jokes**
(https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-office-jokes-dfd55a5a).

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes one one-line office
joke — under 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A colleague replied-all to 4,000 people: 'Please remove me from
> this thread.' By lunch, 312 others had replied-all with the same
> request. It is now our best-attended meeting.

The reply-all spiral, escalated to its logical conclusion: the
thread everyone tried to leave becomes the meeting nobody can.
